Welcome to Cookle 4.0! A fresh look and new ways to collect recipes, enjoy cooking, and remember your meals.

- Explore a refreshed design that makes recipes and meal records easier to browse.
- Import recipes from websites, review and edit the draft, then save it to your collection. Results depend on the source page.
- Follow every cooking step in one view and continue to recording your meal. Find inspiration for today’s meals and browse your Diary by month.
- Choose a favorite recipe to display in your Home Screen widget.
- Export your recipes, Diary, and photos as a Cookle Data file (.cookle). When importing, merge data, choose for each item, or replace all data from a complete-library file.

The data file format has changed. Cookle 4.0 cannot import .cooklebackup files exported by version 3.9. JSON files are also unsupported. This file-format change does not erase the data already stored in the app. After updating, export your current data again in the new format.
